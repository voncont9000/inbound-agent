Review how the assistant introduces itself and whether calls are recorded. Make sure the settings match your organisation's privacy process.

## Review AI disclosure

1. Open the assistant's settings and select **Rules**.
2. Review the **AI disclosure** setting.
3. For WhatsApp, the default setting discloses that the first reply is from the business's AI assistant. The alternative can wait until the customer asks.
4. Changes save automatically.

## Review recording consent

1. Open **Advanced** and review call recording.
2. If recording is on, check the consent message and retention period.
3. Remember that a caller who opts out is not recorded; transcript handling follows the configured setting.

## Keep information in the right workspace

1. Check the workspace name before opening or changing customer information.
2. Use the appropriate workspace role for the work you need to do.
3. For Gmail, review drafts before sending them; the assistant does not send email automatically.

### Good to know

Recordings are automatically deleted when their retention period expires. Ask your administrator if you need help with workspace access or privacy requirements.
