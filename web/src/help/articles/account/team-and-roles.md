Each workspace keeps its assistants and conversation data separate. Your role determines which workspace actions you can take.

## Switch to the right workspace

1. Use the workspace switcher in the app header.
2. Select the workspace you need.
3. Check the workspace name before reviewing or changing its information.

## Understand owner and staff access

- **Owners** manage assistant configuration and channel connections.
- **Staff** can work with conversations, tasks, contacts and knowledge gaps.
- Both roles work within the selected workspace.

### Good to know

Inviting or managing team members from the app is not available yet. Ask your administrator to arrange workspace access.
