The General tab contains the assistant's basic identity and spoken style. Choose details that callers will recognise and test the result before using it.

## Update the assistant's details

1. Open the assistant's settings and select **General**.
2. Set the assistant name and business name.
3. Choose the language and select a voice. The picker starts with recommended voices; use search and the available gender or accent filters, or choose **Show all voices** to browse the catalogue.
4. If you changed the language, use **Translate them to …** beneath the language selector to translate the greeting and call messages in your current draft. Review the result; it is not saved until you choose **Save changes**.
5. Review the greeting and **General** › **Instructions**. This section contains the **Custom prompt** and structured services and intake fields, prices, and contact details.
6. Choose **Save changes**.

## Check how it sounds

1. Start a **Test call**.
2. Listen to the greeting and check pronunciation, language and tone.
3. Return to General settings to adjust anything that needs improvement.

### Good to know

Language availability depends on the options shown in the app. Do not assume that a voice or language is available if it is not listed. For speech speed, see **Advanced** call settings; 1.00× is a natural conversational pace for every voice.

Phone calls start in the assistant's selected language, then reply in the caller's language if the caller speaks another language. Email and WhatsApp replies follow the customer's language when it differs from the selected language. The recording notice is localized for the selected language; multilingual assistants use the English notice.
