Advanced settings cover call behaviour and technical options with template defaults. Change them only when you understand the effect.

## Review call behaviour

1. Open the assistant's settings and select **Advanced**.
2. Review the call limits and silence settings in **Call settings**.
3. Adjust **Speech speed** if needed. 1.00× is a natural conversational pace for every voice; slower Speechify voices are raised toward 175 WPM at this setting. Lower is slower and higher is faster.
4. Review the **Review threshold**. Fields extracted below that confidence are sent to the review queue.

## Review extra live actions

1. Check which extra **Live actions** are enabled, such as flagging urgency, requesting a callback, logging a complaint or ending the call.
2. Booking, forwarding and readback actions are controlled from their cards under **Abilities**, not from this list.
3. Choose **Save changes** and test a representative call.

## Find other ability settings

Readback and caller confirmation are under **Abilities** › **Readback**. Appointment types and booking timing and messages are under **Abilities** › **Calendar booking**. Transfer targets and forwarding messages are under **Abilities** › **Call forwarding**. Keyword and date urgency rules are under **Rules**.

### Good to know

Extra live actions add latency and default to none. The Calendar booking card can turn on missing booking actions; booking also needs a Google Calendar connection. Call forwarding needs at least one configured transfer target.
