Assistant settings are grouped into tabs. Most changes are saved together, while knowledge sources and the knowledge digest have their own save and import controls.

## Find a setting

1. Open [Assistants](/assistants) and select an assistant.
2. Use the tabs **General**, **Rules**, **Knowledge**, **Abilities**, **Channels** and **Advanced** to find the setting you need.
3. Review any validation messages before saving. A tab with errors shows a count beside its name.

**General** holds the assistant's identity, voice and greeting, plus **Instructions** (Custom prompt) with services and intake fields, prices and contact details. **Rules** holds conversation rules and urgency conditions. **Knowledge** holds office hours, FAQs and knowledge sources. **Abilities** holds Calendar booking, Call forwarding, after-conversation actions and Readback. **Channels** shows the assistant's connected channels and links to the workspace Channels page, where owners add, reassign, remove and filter connections. **Advanced** holds other live actions, call settings, review threshold and recording.

## Save assistant settings

1. Make changes in one or more settings tabs.
2. Choose **Save changes** to save the assistant configuration.
3. Check for a success message or fix any validation errors shown on the relevant tab.

### Good to know

Knowledge sources are added or uploaded separately. The digest has its own **Save digest** button, and sources need **Import knowledge** to process them. Version history is not displayed in the settings page.
