Call recording is off by default. If you enable it, the assistant's greeting includes a recording consent message; configure this to fit your organisation's process.

## Configure recording

1. Open the assistant's settings and select **Advanced**.
2. Find the call recording controls and decide whether recording should be enabled.
3. Review the consent message and the recording retention period.
4. Assistant settings save automatically as you edit.

## Play a recording

1. Open [Inbox](/inbox) and select a conversation that has a recording.
2. Find **Call recording** in the conversation details.
3. Use the player to listen. The page shows when the recording is scheduled for automatic deletion.

### Good to know

Recordings are stored for the configured period and deleted automatically when they expire. If a caller opts out of recording, the call is not recorded; transcript handling follows the configured setting.
