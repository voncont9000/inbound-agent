Knowledge helps the assistant answer questions using information supplied by your team. Check it for accuracy and update it when your services or policies change.

## Add business information

1. Open the assistant's settings and select **Knowledge**.
2. Review the frequently asked questions and office hours.
3. Add or edit the information the assistant may share, then choose **Save changes**.

Published prices and contact details are under **General** › **Instructions** (**Custom prompt**), not Knowledge.

## Add and import a knowledge source

1. In **Knowledge sources**, enter a **Website URL** and choose **Add website**, or choose a PDF, DOCX, Markdown or text file and select **Upload file**.
2. Choose **Import knowledge** to process the sources.
3. Review the import status and check the resulting information for accuracy.

## Review the knowledge digest

1. Check **Knowledge digest** after importing sources.
2. Edit **Digest content** where needed, staying within the character limit shown below the field.
3. Choose **Save digest**; it saves separately from the assistant's other settings.

### Good to know

Adding a source does not itself import it. The digest has a separate save button, and an import may report that a website returned no readable text.
