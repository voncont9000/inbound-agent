Rules tell the assistant how to handle conversations. Use clear, specific directions and check them with a test call.

## Choose rule presets

1. Open the assistant's settings and select **Rules**.
2. Review the available rule presets and choose the ones that fit how your team works.
3. Add or edit custom rules when you need a behaviour that a preset does not cover.
4. Choose **Save changes**.

## Set pronunciation and urgency guidance

1. Add pronunciation guidance for names or terms the assistant may say aloud.
2. Review the urgency guidance and specify what the team should treat as urgent.
3. Review **Keyword and date urgency rules** in the Rules tab alongside the written urgency guidance.
4. Save your changes and make a **Test call** using an example scenario.

### Good to know

Rules do not replace accurate business knowledge. Add factual answers in Knowledge settings and keep instructions focused on behaviour.
