Before callers or message senders use an assistant, check its details, knowledge, abilities and channel configuration. Ask your administrator about channel routing or deployment setup.

## Check the assistant

1. Review the assistant's [settings](/assistants), including its language, voice, greeting and rules.
2. Confirm that its knowledge is current and does not contain sample information.
3. Check that only the abilities you intend to use are enabled.

## Test the experience

1. Make a **Test call** and try representative questions.
2. Review the conversation and correct any inaccurate answers or intake details.
3. Check any enabled booking, transfer or after-conversation action configuration.

## Check each channel

1. Confirm the phone number or messaging connection is associated with the intended assistant.
2. For Gmail, remember that the assistant creates drafts for a person to review; it does not send them.
3. For WhatsApp, make sure your team understands that replies outside the 24-hour messaging window need a template, which the app does not provide.
4. Ask your administrator to check any channel routing that is managed outside the app.

### Good to know

Test calls do not run after-conversation actions. Check real conversations and channel-specific settings separately.
