Your account gives you access to a workspace, where assistants and conversations are kept separate from other organisations. Use the workspace switcher if your account belongs to more than one workspace.

## Create your account

1. Open `/signup` and enter `Email address`, `Password` (at least 12 characters), `Your name` and `Workspace name`.
2. Select `Create account` to create your account and workspace.
3. To return later, open `/login`, enter `Email address` and `Password`, then select `Sign in`.

## Switch workspaces

1. Open the workspace switcher in the app header.
2. Choose the workspace you want to work in.
3. Check the selected workspace before changing assistant settings or reviewing conversations.

### Good to know

Workspace data is kept separate. Ask your administrator if you need access to another workspace.
