Templates provide a starting configuration for an assistant. **Blank** is selected by default for new assistants. The six choices are **Blank**, **UK B2C law firm**, **Restaurant**, **Dental or medical clinic**, **Home services and trades**, and **Real estate and lettings**.

## Choose a template

1. Open [Assistants](/assistants) and choose **Set up from website** to open the template picker.
2. Leave **Blank** selected for a general starting point, or choose an industry template.
3. Enter the business details and continue through setup.

## Create a demo assistant

Templates with sample business details have a **Create demo assistant** button. It creates an assistant filled with that template's sample data, which is marked for replacement. Replace the sample business details with accurate information before going live.

## Personalise the assistant

1. Open the assistant's [settings](/assistants).
2. Update its name, language, voice, greeting and instructions.
3. Check its rules, knowledge and enabled abilities.
4. Use **Save changes** after reviewing the settings.

## Delete assistants

Owners can delete an assistant from the Assistants overview. The overview shows how many conversations it has. Deleting an assistant with conversations permanently deletes those conversations, including transcripts and recordings; contacts are kept. An assistant with an active conversation cannot be deleted until that conversation has finished.

### Good to know

Templates are starting points, not a complete description of your organisation. Check every name, service, policy and response before going live. A demo assistant contains sample business details; replace them before use.
