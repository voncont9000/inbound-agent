Templates provide a starting configuration for an assistant. Choose one that is close to the work you need, then replace sample details with information that is accurate for your organisation.

## Choose a template

1. Open [Assistants](/assistants) and start creating an assistant.
2. Choose **Create from template**.
3. Select a suitable template and enter the requested assistant details.
4. Review the generated setup before using the assistant.

## Personalise the assistant

1. Open the assistant's [settings](/assistants).
2. Update its name, language, voice, greeting and instructions.
3. Check its rules, knowledge and enabled abilities.
4. Use **Save changes** after reviewing the settings.

### Good to know

Templates are starting points, not a complete description of your organisation. Check every name, service, policy and response before going live.
