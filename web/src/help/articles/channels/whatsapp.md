WhatsApp connects through the phone number ID provided for the account. The assistant identifies itself as AI in its first reply and can handle supported text and media context.

## Connect a WhatsApp channel

1. Open the assistant's settings and select **Channels**.
2. Choose the WhatsApp connection option and provide the phone number ID and other connection details.
3. Save the assistant settings and check that the connection is shown for the intended assistant.
4. Ask your administrator if you do not have the connection details.

## Understand the messaging window

1. Review the time of the most recent customer message before expecting a reply.
2. Within the 24-hour customer service window, the assistant can respond according to its settings.
3. Outside that window, a template is required. The app creates a template-needed task rather than sending a templated reply.
4. Review conversations to see how images, audio or other media were represented as context.

### Good to know

The app does not provide embedded WhatsApp sign-up or a template editor. Calendar booking can be used in supported live interactions when the relevant ability is configured.
