Phone routing must be arranged for your workspace. The app does not provide a number-buying flow; ask your administrator to configure the phone service and route the number to the assistant.

## Add a phone channel

1. Open the assistant's settings and select **Channels**.
2. Choose **Add a channel manually**.
3. Select the phone channel type and enter the phone number your administrator provided.
4. Choose **Add channel**. The channel is saved straight away; you don't need **Save changes**.

## Check the connection

1. Confirm with your administrator that calls to the number are routed to this assistant.
2. Make a **Test call** to check the assistant's greeting and answers.
3. If a real call does not arrive, ask your administrator to check the number routing.

### Good to know

The number and its connection to the phone service are managed outside the assistant setup flow. Do not change routing details unless your administrator has provided them.
