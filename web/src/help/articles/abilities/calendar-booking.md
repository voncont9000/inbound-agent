Appointment booking uses Google Calendar. The assistant offers configured appointment slots and creates an event only after the caller selects and confirms a slot.

## Configure an appointment type

1. Open the assistant's settings and select **Abilities**.
2. Enable appointment booking and add the appointment types your team offers.
3. Review each type's duration and when it should be used.
4. Connect Google Calendar from the **Calendar booking** card. Connecting saves your settings first and returns you to **Abilities**.

## Enable live booking

1. In **Abilities** › **Calendar booking**, enable the ability. If the card says booking actions are missing, choose **Turn them on** there.
2. Review **Booking timing and messages** in the same card, including minimum notice, booking horizon, buffer and maximum slots offered.
3. Assistant settings save automatically as you edit. Connecting Google Calendar saves your settings first.
4. Make a **Test call** to check how the assistant offers and confirms appointments.

### Good to know

Booking does not reschedule or cancel existing events. Gmail does not book appointments. The app supports Google Calendar only; ask your administrator if Calendar is not available for your deployment.
