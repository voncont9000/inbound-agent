Knowledge gaps collect questions the assistant could not answer confidently. Review the linked conversation, then update the assistant's knowledge or dismiss the gap when no change is needed.

## Review an open gap

1. Open [Knowledge gaps](/knowledge-gaps).
2. Filter by **Status** or **Assistant**.
3. Read the question and evidence, then choose **View conversation** to see its context.
4. Add a reliable answer in the assistant's Knowledge settings if the information should be available next time.
5. Choose **Mark answered** after adding the answer, or choose **Dismiss** if no knowledge change is needed.

## Reopen a gap

1. Set the status filter to **Answered** or **Dismissed**.
2. Find the question and choose **Reopen** if it needs more work.

### Good to know

Updating a gap's status and adding business knowledge are separate actions. Check that the answer is accurate before marking a question answered.
