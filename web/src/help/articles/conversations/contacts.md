Contact profiles are built from information associated with conversations across supported channels. They bring together identities, known facts, conversations and a context card.

## Find a contact

1. Open [Contacts](/contacts).
2. Search for the person and open their profile.
3. Review their identities, active facts, conversations, open tasks and context card.

## Merge or split profiles

1. On a contact profile, choose another contact under **Merge another contact into this one**.
2. Choose **Merge contact** and confirm after checking which profile will remain.
3. To separate identities later, choose **Split selected identities** and select the identities to move.

### Good to know

Merges are audited and can be reversed through a split. Check identities and conversation links before changing contact profiles.
