The [Home dashboard](/) summarises activity for the selected workspace and date range. Use it to review conversation volume and outcomes alongside bookings, tasks, response time and estimated costs.

## Choose the dashboard range

1. Open **Home**.
2. Choose a date range such as today, the last 7 days or the last 30 days, or set a custom range.
3. Check the displayed timezone and any assistant filter before comparing totals.

## Understand the measures

- **Conversations** counts conversations that started in the selected range. Test calls are excluded by default.
- **Passed to staff** includes conversations with follow-up tasks, operator replies or a completed/answered/simulated call transfer. Other counted conversations are treated as handled by the assistant.
- **Bookings** counts booked Calendar appointments linked to counted conversations. **Tasks** counts non-system tasks, and **Callbacks** counts callback tasks.
- **Estimated time saved** uses an estimate per channel, with a lower credit when a conversation was passed to staff.
- **Median response time** is measured for calls with recorded turn-latency data.
- **Cost** is split into telephony and AI estimates.

### Good to know

Dashboard figures are estimates and depend on the available conversation and call metrics. Changing the date range or timezone can change the totals and chart buckets.
