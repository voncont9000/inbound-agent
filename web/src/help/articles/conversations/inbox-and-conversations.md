The inbox brings together conversations for the selected workspace. Open a conversation to review its transcript, intake details and any channel-specific information.

Calls show one row per turn, with a clear speaker label and the time elapsed since the call started.

## Find a conversation

1. Open [Inbox](/inbox).
2. Use the available filters to narrow the list, including **Needs review** where applicable.
3. Select a conversation to open its details.

## Review conversation details

1. Read the summary and transcript.
2. Compare the live, post-call and merged intake values.
3. If a field needs correction, open its review control, enter the right value and choose **Submit review**.
4. Check any email triage, transfer, appointment booking, task or recording panels shown for the conversation.

### Good to know

Test calls are marked **Test call** and do not count towards Home dashboard conversation totals. Gmail replies appear as drafts for human review.
