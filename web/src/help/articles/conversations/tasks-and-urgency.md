Tasks record follow-up work such as callbacks, urgent concerns, complaints or requests for a person to respond. The task kind and details explain what needs attention.

## Find and update a task

1. Open [Tasks](/tasks).
2. Filter by **Status**, **Kind** or **Assistant**.
3. Review the task details, source and due date.
4. Change its status to **open**, **done** or **dismissed** as appropriate.

## Adjust urgency handling

1. Open the assistant's settings and select **Rules** to review written urgency guidance.
2. In **Rules**, review the **Keyword and date urgency rules** section.
3. Add a **Keyword** rule or a **Date deadline** rule and specify its reason.
4. Choose **Save changes** and test an example.

### Good to know

Urgency rules are configured per assistant. Keep task statuses current so your team can distinguish open follow-up from completed or dismissed work.
