Clear details help your administrator find the relevant conversation and understand what happened. Share only information that is appropriate for your organisation.

## Gather the details

1. Copy the conversation link from the app, if the issue relates to a conversation.
2. Note the date and approximate time, including the timezone.
3. Record the channel and assistant involved.
4. Describe what you expected to happen and what actually happened.
5. List the steps that reproduce the issue and include any message shown in the app.

## Send the report

1. Share the details with your administrator or the support contact for your workspace.
2. Include a relevant screenshot only if it helps explain the issue and your organisation permits it.
3. Do not include passwords, access tokens or other secrets.

### Good to know

For a call or message, include the conversation link rather than copying more customer information than necessary.
