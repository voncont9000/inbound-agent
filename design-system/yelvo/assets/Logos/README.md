The Yelvo logo: the lowercase wordmark set in Bricolage Grotesque 800 (outlined), ending in the speaking dot, an ink disc holding three yellow sound bars.

- `yelvo-wordmark.svg`: ink letters and dot with yellow bars. For `surface`, `surface-secondary` and `accent` grounds.
- `yelvo-wordmark-on-dark.svg`: white letters, yellow dot, ink bars. For ink and dark grounds.
- `yelvo-mark.svg`: the speaking dot alone (ink with yellow bars). Use below 80px wide and as an avatar.
- `yelvo-app-icon.svg`: yellow rounded square with the ink dot. The default app icon and favicon.
- `yelvo-app-icon-dark.svg`: ink rounded square with a yellow dot. Only on light docks and wallpapers.

Inks are fixed (#111111, #ffffff, #ffd60a) and do not follow the theme.
